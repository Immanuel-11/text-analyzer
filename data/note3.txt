LLM is large language model. llm is used to understand and work with human language. llm is trained with a huge amount of data.Large Language Model (LLM) is a specific, advanced subset of modern AI that belongs inside the field of NLP. LLMs (such as GPT-4 or Claude) use massive deep-learning transformer architectures and billions of parameters to generate fluent, human-like text and perform open-ended reasoning.
